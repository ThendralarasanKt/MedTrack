Live Prototype Capabilities

Try In Mockup

Every screen in the phone simulation is fully dynamic and interconnected. You can perform real clinical workflows:

1. Interactive Inpatient Census Type into the search bar to filter patients in real time. Switch tabs (Primary, Referral, Discharged) to test the census filter. Click any patient card to open their full clinical hub.
2. Actionable Rounds Queue Click "Done + Note" on any pending task. It immediately marks the task complete, deducts the pending badge, and updates the census in memory!
3. Human-in-the-Loop AI Gate Go to "AI Gate", click "Confirm & Commit". Watch the four staged atomic actions commit to the patient's record and appear in the timeline!
4. Modals & Bed Transfers Click "+ Admit" (top right) to admit a new patient, or "Move Bed" on the patient hub to simulate transferring a patient between wards.
